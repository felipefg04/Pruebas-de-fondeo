FAQ

Where do the rules come from?

The "2-step" and "1-step" options summarize the most common evaluations as of June 2026: 10% and 5% targets, 5% or 3% daily loss and 10% total loss. Every firm has its own details: use "Custom" and confirm them on the firm's site before paying.

What does "luck alone" mean?

We simulate the same strategy with the same volatility but an average profit of zero. That's what trading with no edge looks like. If your result looks like the luck result, the challenge comes down to chance.

Do you store my data?

No. Everything runs in your browser; your numbers and trades are never sent to any server.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's a calculation tool. Past results don't guarantee future results, and leveraged trading carries a risk of loss.

How we calculate: 2,000 simulated evaluations per result, day by day and trade by trade, against the target, the daily loss (measured from each day's starting balance) and the total loss. Intraday open P&L isn't modeled, so the real daily-loss risk is somewhat higher.
