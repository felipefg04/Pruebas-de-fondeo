Prop firm challenge pass rate: what are your odds?

Most attempts fail. But that's everyone's average. Your odds depend on three things you can measure before you pay.

The average isn't your number

Some firms have published their numbers, and they're low: one reported that only 1 in 20 traders pass, another that 6% reached funded status in one month. That average mixes traders with no plan and traders with a tested strategy. What matters is how often your strategy would pass under your rules.

  1. Your edgeWin rate and average win in R. With no edge, the challenge is a coin flip.
  2. Your risk per tradeToo low: you don't hit the target in time. Too high: the daily loss limit ends it.
  3. The rulesTarget, daily loss, max loss (static or trailing) and minimum days.

A simulated example

Strategy: 45% win rate, 1.6R average win, 1.5 trades per day. 2-step evaluation (10% and 5% targets, 5% daily loss, 10% static max loss), up to 90 trading days per step. 4,000 simulations per row.

0.5%59%5%Runs out of time (39%)

2%40%19%Daily loss (45%)

The same strategy passes 76% or 40% of the time just by changing risk. And a strategy with no edge (50% win rate, 1R average win) passes about as often as luck: around 22% at 1% risk.

These are simulations, not real results. They don't include intraday open P&L, commissions or slippage, so reality is usually somewhat worse.

FAQ

What risk per trade is best for a challenge?

It depends on your strategy. In the example, the best point is around 1%. The calculator shows the full curve for your numbers.

Can you pass by luck?

Sometimes. With no edge and 1% risk, about 1 in 5 attempts passes by chance. If your odds look like luck's, paying for the challenge is a bet.

Are my firm's rules different?

Probably in some detail. Use "Custom" in the calculator and confirm the rules on the firm's site before paying.
